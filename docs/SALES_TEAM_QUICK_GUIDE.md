```
🎯 WHAT IT MEANS: Long-term potential
⏱️ WHEN TO CONTACT: Next month in batch nurture
💭 ACTION: Add to nurture campaign
📊 SUCCESS RATE: ~10-15% closing rate

- ✅ Don't waste time on manual outreach

- ✅ Monitor for engagement signals

```
🎯 WHAT IT MEANS: Not a fit (now)
⏱️ WHEN TO CONTACT: Not recommended

📊 SUCCESS RATE: <5% closing rate

**Your checklist**:
- ✅ Don't waste sales time
- ✅ Archive from active pipeline
- ✅ Keep in database for future reference
- ✅ Delete if clearly spam
- ✅ Only revisit if company grows

## 🎓 Understanding the Scores

### What Each Score Dimension Means

**Company Fit** (25% of score):
- "Is this the type of company we help?"
- ✅ High: Stripe, HubSpot, Asana (known software companies)
- ❌ Low: Solo freelancer, brand new unknown startup

**Budget** (25% of score):
- "Can they afford our solution?"
- ✅ High: "$50k approved budget", "VP decision authority"
- ❌ Low: "Freelancer", "Still evaluating cost"

**Pain Points** (30% of score - most important):
- "Do they have problems we solve?"
- ✅ High: "Need to automate manual processes", "Growing too fast"
- ❌ Low: "Curious about new tools", "Just browsing"

**Contact Quality** (20% of score):
- "Is this person legit and reachable?"
- ✅ High: john@acmecorp.com + phone + full name
- ❌ Low: temp email, no phone, first name only

## 🚨 When Score Seems Wrong

**Lead looks HOT but score is WARM?**
```
Check:
□ Missing company information? Add it!
□ No phone number? Request it.
□ Pain points only vague? Add details if known.
□ Budget not mentioned? Ask during call.

→ System will re-score when data is updated
```

**Lead should be COLD but score is HOT?**
```
Check:
□ Did they mention specific pain? (system scored it high)
□ Did they indicate real budget? (system weighted it)
□ Are they from known company? (system trusted it)

→ This is why you call first - verify before investing time
```

## 🎯 Sales Tips by Category

### For HOT Leads (80-100)
1. **Speed matters** - Call same day, not next week
2. **Prepare personalized demo** - Reference their specific pain
3. **Have pricing ready** - They may ask to close same call
4. **Get commitment** - Don't leave without next step
5. **Document everything** - These become case studies

### For WARM Leads (60-79)
1. **Build relationship first** - Don't jump to sales
2. **Provide value** - Send relevant content, not pitches
3. **Be patient** - May take 3-5 touches
4. **Ask questions** - Understand their situation better
5. **Create urgency gently** - "Special offer expires..."

### For COLD Leads (40-59)
1. **Let automation do it** - Nurture email sequences
2. **Set reminders** - Re-score in 30 days
3. **Assign lower priority** - Don't interrupt hot lead work
4. **Monitor engagement** - Link clicks = warm lead signal
5. **Archive after 90 days** - If no engagement, delete

### For UNQUALIFIED Leads (<40)
1. **Don't contact manually** - Not a good use of time
2. **Delete if clearly spam** - Cleaner database
3. **Archive if company might grow** - Revisit in 1 year
4. **Learn from patterns** - Why keep getting poor leads?
5. **Adjust intake process** - Add qualifying form questions

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I ignore the score and just call whoever I want?**
A: Yes, but the score saves you time. Focus HOT first, then WARM.

**Q: What if a COLD lead picks up and wants to talk?**
A: Perfect! They escalated themselves. Follow through - they may become WARM/HOT.

**Q: How often does my score change?**
A: When you add new information to their profile (company, budget, pain points, etc.)

**Q: Can I request re-scoring for a lead?**
A: Yes - update their profile with new info, then request "force_requalify"

**Q: What's the AI actually doing?**
A: Analyzing 4 dimensions: company fit, budget, pain points, contact quality.

**Q: Can I dispute a score?**
A: Yes - the reasoning tab shows exactly why. If data is wrong, update it.

**Q: Should I trust this score?**
A: Use it as guide, not gospel. Your judgment + AI = best results.

**Q: What if I'm not seeing HOT leads?**
A: Either your leads need more info, or they're genuinely not high-fit. 
   Both are valuable insights for marketing to fix.
